Password recovery is available through the “Forgot Password” option on the login screen.

Users can reset their password using their registered mobile number or email address.

Debit cards can be used for ATM withdrawals, online purchases, and in-store payments.

Credit cards allow users to spend up to a predefined credit limit and repay later through monthly billing cycles.

Card transactions may fail due to insufficient balance, exceeded credit limit, expired card, incorrect PIN, or temporary network issues.

One-time passwords (OTP) are required for most online transactions and may be delayed due to network or carrier issues.

Fund transfers can be made between accounts using UPI, bank transfer, or card-based payments.

UPI payments may fail due to bank server downtime, incorrect UPI ID, daily transaction limits, or connectivity issues.

Account balance and recent transactions are available on the account summary page.

Transaction history typically shows completed, pending, and failed transactions.

Loan services may include personal loans, home loans, education loans, or vehicle loans.

Loan eligibility depends on factors such as income, credit score, repayment history, and existing liabilities.

Loan applications may take time to process due to verification and approval checks.

Loan repayment schedules and outstanding balances are available in the loans section of the app.

EMI payments can fail due to insufficient account balance or technical issues during auto-debit.

Card blocking and unblocking options are available in the card management section.

Users can update personal details such as address and contact information from the profile section.

Security notifications are sent when there are new logins, password changes, or suspicious activities.

The banking application may undergo maintenance, during which some services can be temporarily unavailable.

Customer support is available for issues related to account access, transactions, cards, and loans.
